MODEL B2B · USERS supplier companies, finance and procurement teams · TECH SAP S/4HANA, MDM, OutSystems

Digital Account Opening

Itaú Unibanco · 2015 – 2016

MODEL B2C · USERS new retail banking customers · TECH OCR, biometrics, antifraud and compliance services

Challenge

Opening an account required a branch visit. The bank needed a remote flow that still met identity, compliance and antifraud requirements.

Product

An end-to-end mobile onboarding journey with document capture (OCR) and biometric identity checks.

Decisions

  • Designed the journey ahead of the Central Bank rules that later regulated digital accounts.
  • Released first to selected leads in a controlled launch, then opened to the public.

Dependencies

Compliance, legal, fraud prevention and core-banking teams.

Engineering Lifecycle Platform

BERD · 2025 – 2026

MODEL B2B SaaS · USERS engineering teams, 5 beta customers · TECH web platform built by 3 vendors; ChatGPT and Claude in product workflows

Challenge

Thirteen workstreams and three delivery vendors had to converge on one product, with no shared standard for findings, UI copy or acceptance.

Product

A SaaS platform for the engineering lifecycle, released to beta customers and launched in November 2026.

Decisions

  • Set six operating standards for QA, findings and acceptance.
  • Tracked 70+ findings through UAT to closure.
  • Merged three Figma environments into one and standardised 197 UI strings across ~40 screens.
  • Built 24 AI-assisted skills for requirements, documentation and QA.

Team & dependencies

13-person delivery team across 3 vendors, 15 stakeholders, 29 user flows, 42 components.

16h → 3hto produce a UAT report13workstreams aligned (9 functional, 4 cross-cutting)

Itaú Unibanco · 2017

MODEL B2C · USERS card and banking customers · TECH conversational AI on WhatsApp and in-app, service-centre integration

Product

One assistant on two channels: WhatsApp and the in-app virtual assistant (AVI), with authentication and handover to a human agent.

Decisions

Defined intents, training data and conversation flows, set escalation rules, and ran output QA before each release.

Dependencies

Service centre, security and channel teams.

How I work

Practices I bring to a team

Business case before backlog

I size the operational cost first, then decide scope. Partner Connect was funded on a business case I shaped.

Discovery in waves

Short discovery rounds with the people who carry the manual work, so scope follows evidence rather than the loudest request.

Integrations as product

ERP, master data, identity and service-centre integrations are part of the user experience. I write them into the journey from day one.

Quality you can audit

Shared standards for findings, acceptance and UI copy, so several vendors ship like one team.

Applied AI with guardrails

Conversational products (intents, training data, flows, output QA) and AI-assisted workflows for requirements, documentation and QA.

Adoption is the outcome

Launch plans, change management and metrics that show whether people actually switched.
